Working abroad — FAQ

Common questions about international recruitment and document requirements.

What is the difference between skilled and non-skilled applications?

Skilled roles typically require qualifications, certifications, or specialised experience (e.g. engineering, IT, healthcare). Non-skilled roles focus on operational, hospitality, logistics, and general labour positions. Both pathways use the same application process.

Which documents do I need to work in another country?

Requirements vary by destination. Generally you will need a valid passport, appropriate visa or work permit, and sometimes police clearance, medical certificates, and credential assessments. Our document step shows a checklist based on your country of residence.

Can I apply if I do not have all documents yet?

Yes. You can indicate “In progress” for document status fields. However, employers may not proceed until required documents are complete. Upload what you have and add notes in the additional information section.

How long does the recruitment process take?

Timelines depend on the role, country, and visa processing. After submission, our team reviews your application and typically responds within 5–10 business days.

Is my personal information secure?

Applications are sent securely to our recruitment team via email and only used for recruitment purposes. Do not share passwords or sensitive banking details in the form.

Who receives my application?

On the review page, click Open email app & send application. Your email app opens with the full application addressed to workschengenhr@gmail.com. Attach your documents and press Send in your email app to complete submission.
